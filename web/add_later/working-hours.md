# IT Service Desk Hours

## Standard support
The IT service desk operates Monday to Friday, 08:30 to 17:30 UK time, excluding public holidays.

## Priority incidents
For a complete loss of business-critical systems outside these hours, telephone the on-call number listed on the company intranet. Routine issues should be logged as a ticket and will be reviewed during service desk hours.

## Tickets
Include your name, device asset number where appropriate, a short description of the problem and any relevant error messages. Never include passwords or MFA codes.
